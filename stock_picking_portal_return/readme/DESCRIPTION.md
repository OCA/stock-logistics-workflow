Brings the return features of the sales order portal to the stock operations
portal:

- Delivered operations get a **Return** button, in the list of operations and
  on the page of the operation, giving the customer the return slip to put
  inside the parcel sent back.
- The page of a delivery lists its returns with their date and status:
  awaiting arrival, received or cancelled.
- The page of a return links back to the delivery it comes from.

Operations whose type is not published in the portal are shown by name,
without a link.
