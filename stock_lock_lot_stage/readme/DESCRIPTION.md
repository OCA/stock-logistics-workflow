Manage lot lock status through configurable stages, from pending quality
control to approved for usage, or rejected.

This is useful in regulated environments meeting Good Manufacturing Practices
(GMP) requirements, where each item must be quality controlled and checked
before being usable.

GMP is relevant for businesses such as the manufacture and sale of food and
beverages, cosmetics, pharmaceutical products, dietary supplements, and
medical devices.

This module extends the
[Stock Lock Lot](https://odoo-community.org/shop/stock-lock-lot-4264) module,
adding stage-based management of the lot blocked status.
