This module makes the effective date (*Date Done*) of stock transfers
and scraps editable and auditable.

Odoo 19 already lets you edit the effective date of a done transfer and
propagates it to the related stock moves and move lines. On top of that,
this module adds:

-  **Original Effective Date** (``origin_date_done``) on transfers and
   scraps: the real processing timestamp, kept as an audit reference
   after the effective date is edited.
-  **A permission group** ("Modify Effective Date on Done Records")
   required to edit the effective date of done records. Dates in a
   hard-locked fiscal period cannot be changed, for transfers and scraps
   alike.
-  **Editable scrap date** (read-only and stamped with the validation
   time in standard Odoo), propagated to the scrap move and its line.
-  **Pre-set effective date**: a date entered before validation is kept,
   and the stock journal entry is dated on it.

This module changes the date only, not the inventory valuation. For
foreign-currency purchase receipts, also install
```purchase_stock_date_done_revaluation`` <https://github.com/OCA/purchase-workflow/tree/19.0/purchase_stock_date_done_revaluation>`__
so that the value follows the edited date's exchange rate.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

-  When migrating from ``stock_move_actual_date``, the legacy
   ``actual_date`` is a local date, but the timezone it was entered in
   is not recorded. The migration assumes the timezone of the record's
   company (UTC if the company has none) to convert it to the UTC
   effective date. Records entered by users in a different timezone than
   their company may end up a day off and should be checked after the
   migration.
-  Editing the effective date of a done record does not change the date
   of journal entries already posted, unlike ``stock_move_actual_date``.
