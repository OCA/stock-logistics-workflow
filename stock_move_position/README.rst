Adds a stable line reference on stock moves, numbered 1, 2, 3, ...: the
**position** of the move in its transfer.

Positions are renumbered automatically when moves are added to or
removed from a transfer. A split move keeps the position of the original
one. The moves sorted by position follow the numbers (10 after 9).

The numbering comes from ``base_line_numbering``. The position of the
moves in their batch transfer is added by
``stock_picking_batch_move_position``.

Use Cases / Context
===================

Transfers are often exchanged with third parties, such as a warehouse
management system (WMS) or a carrier, which refer to the lines by a
position and send it back in their confirmations. Such a reference must
not change once it has been sent, unlike the ``sequence`` used to order
the lines.

``stock_picking_line_sequence`` also numbers the moves of a transfer,
with an integer used to order them. This module gives stable references
instead.

Usage
=====

The position is shown on the operations of a transfer.

Positions are kept up to date automatically. To renumber them
explicitly, use the action **Renumber positions** on a transfer
(Inventory managers).

Modules sending positions to a third party can prevent the automatic
renumbering once a record has been sent, by overriding
``_line_renumber_needed()`` (see ``base_line_numbering``).
