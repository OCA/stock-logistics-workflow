Log in as a portal user. There will be a new "Move operations" menu in
the list of available documents.

The list shows every individual stock movement (incoming or outgoing)
linked to your pickings, with:

- Document name (number)
- Movement date
- Movement type (Receipt/Delivery)
- Product
- Quantity
- State

Open a document. Following information is shown on the page:

- Document name (number)
- Document date
- Delivery address
- List of items:
  - Product name
  - Quantity
