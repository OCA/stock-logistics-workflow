Adds the delivery carrier tracking reference to the stock operations
customer portal, rendered as a clickable link whenever the carrier
provides a tracking URL.

The tracking information is shown in two places:

- As a "Tracking" column in the stock operations list.
- As a "Tracking" block on the stock operation page, next to the
  shipping date.

Carriers returning several tracking numbers for a single operation
(multi parcel shipments) are rendered as one link per parcel.
