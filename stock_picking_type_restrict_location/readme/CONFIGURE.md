To restrict the locations of an operation type:

1.  Enable *Storage Locations* in *Inventory \> Configuration \> Settings*.
2.  Go to *Inventory \> Configuration \> Warehouse Management \> Operations
    Types* and open the operation type.
3.  Check its **Source Location** and **Destination Location**: they are the
    locations its transfers will be restricted to. A vendor or customer
    location is not restricted.
4.  Enable **Restrict Location**.

The option cannot be enabled, and the locations of a restricted operation type
cannot be changed, while transfers in progress or rules of that operation type
use other locations. The message names the transfers, or the first rule, to
finish, cancel or correct first. The option can always be disabled.

The **Unrestricted Locations** filter of the operation types list shows the
ones that are not restricted.
