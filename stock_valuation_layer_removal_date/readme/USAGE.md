Go to Inventory > Reporting > Valuation, and use Removal Date to filter the
valuation layers, either from the search panel or with a custom filter. The
field can also be displayed as an optional column of the valuation list, and
used to sort or group the layers.

The removal date of a layer follows the lots of its move, so it is realigned
automatically when the removal date of a lot changes, or when the lots of the
move themselves change.
