When the *Email Confirmation* option of Inventory is enabled, Odoo sends
the delivery confirmation email while the transfer is being validated.
Rendering the delivery slip attached to that email can take a few
seconds, and the user has to wait for it before the validation finishes.

This module posts that email in a queue job instead, so the validation
does not wait for the PDF. Everything else done when a transfer is
validated (for example, the shipping label requested to the carrier)
still runs as usual.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Inventory > Configuration > Settings* and enable *Email
   Confirmation*.
2. Validate a delivery order.
3. A job *Send confirmation email of ...* is created. Once it is done,
   the email with the delivery slip appears in the chatter of the
   transfer.
