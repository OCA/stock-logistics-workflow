This module stores the picking partner on stock move lines and exposes it in
the stock move line list and search views.

It allows users to:

* display the picking partner in moves history;
* search stock move lines by picking partner;
* group stock move lines by picking partner.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

Go to Inventory > Reporting > Moves History.

You can:

* display the Picking Partner column in the list;
* search by picking partner;
* filter records with a picking partner;
* group records by picking partner.
