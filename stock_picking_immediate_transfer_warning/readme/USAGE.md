1. Go to *Inventory \> Operations* and open a transfer that has reserved
   quantities (state "Ready").
2. Without marking any move line as "Picked", click the *Validate* button.
3. A warning wizard will appear informing you that no quantities have been
   explicitly picked and that Odoo will process all reserved quantities.
4. Click *Process* to proceed with the validation or *Discard* to cancel.
