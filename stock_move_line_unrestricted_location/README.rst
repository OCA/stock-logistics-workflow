In standard Odoo behavior for pickings, the selection of source and
destination locations is constrained by the warehouse configuration.
Users can typically only choose the default locations defined on the
picking type (incoming, internal, or outgoing) and their direct
hierarchical child locations.

Universal module to expand stock move location selection, allowing all
child locations of the warehouse view location. Works for Incoming,
Internal, and Outgoing pickings while keeping standard filters.
